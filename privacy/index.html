Privacy Policy

Talk Studio LLC · Effective October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Talk Studio LLC ("Talk Studio," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit talkstudio.space and its subdomains, use our software and marketing services for real estate professionals and local businesses, or communicate with us by phone, email, or text message.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, business name, email address, mobile phone number, mailing address, and any details you share when you contact us, request a demo, create an account, or opt in to text messages.
  • Account and service data: information you enter into our software, such as contacts, property records, review requests, and campaign settings.
  • Payment information: processed by our third-party payment processor. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Automatically collected data: device and browser type, IP address, pages visited, and similar usage data collected through cookies and analytics tools.
  • Communication records: records of calls, emails, and text messages exchanged with us, including your SMS opt-in and opt-out history.

How we use your information

  • To provide, operate, and support our software and services
  • To respond to inquiries, schedule demos and appointments, and provide customer support
  • To send account, onboarding, and service notifications
  • To send marketing messages about Talk Studio products and offers when you have opted in
  • To process payments and prevent fraud or abuse
  • To improve our website and services and comply with legal obligations

SMS / text messaging

If you opt in to receive text messages from Talk Studio, for example by texting a keyword such as INFO to (319) 449-3178 or by checking an SMS consent box on our website, we collect your mobile number and a record of your consent. We use that number only to send the messages you agreed to receive: replies to your inquiries, appointment and account updates, customer support, and occasional promotional offers from Talk Studio.

Talk Studio does not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All categories of information sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and get help by replying HELP. See our SMS Terms for details.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With service providers that help us run our business, such as hosting, payment processing, email delivery, and our messaging platform provider, who may use it only to perform services for us (this does not include sharing SMS opt-in data for anyone's marketing purposes)
  • When required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Talk Studio, our customers, or others
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets, subject to this policy
  • With your consent

Customer data in our software

When businesses use Talk Studio software to manage their own contacts, those businesses control that data. We process it on their behalf to provide the service and do not use it for our own marketing.

Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep our site working, remember preferences, and understand how visitors use our site. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law, including keeping records of SMS consent and opt-outs. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

  • Opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any email.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us below.

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
